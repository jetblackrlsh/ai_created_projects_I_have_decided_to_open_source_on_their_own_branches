# World and art direction

The Loomways let sovereign dimensions share light, memory and passage without surrendering their identities. Seven adult fairy custodians form a voluntary Accord when rival magical conquerors seize seven junctions. Each victory returns a stolen civic freedom and opens the next route. Their final enemy is a living treaty that offers peace by removing everyone's choice. They must dismantle its claim without destroying the connections on which the realms depend.

## Character design briefs

The briefs below guide original illustrations; generated results can vary in small details.

### Tavra — Dragon Fire

Home: Emberkiln. Visual brief: adult stocky dark-skinned woman, silver buzz cut, amber eyes, ivory ceramic shoulder armor over charcoal full-length artisan coat and wide trousers, glowing amber kiln vents, four broad translucent smoke-glass rectangular wings, ceramic lantern gauntlets; warm gold and charcoal palette; magical dragon-shaped flame, not a literal dragon companion.

### Oru — Sun & Moon Light

Home: Parallax Arcade. Visual brief: adult androgynous fairy with tawny skin, long black braided crown, round spectacles, cobalt layered traveling robe and silver mosaic sash, six asymmetrical diamond-mirror wings, a brass sun-and-moon astrolabe; golden sunlight and silver moonlight magic; cobalt and reflective silver palette.

### Veska — Nature & Flora

Home: The Undergarden. Visual brief: adult broad-shouldered olive-skinned woman, short white curls, copper freckles, plum quilted field jacket, sturdy cream trousers and boots, translucent fan-shaped veined leaf-fan wings, copper branch staff with flowers; plants, vines and blossoms magic; violet copper and pearl palette.

### Pella — Soundwaves & Harmony

Home: The Resonant Steps. Visual brief: adult brown-skinned woman, shaved side haircut with thick golden top curls, navy ceremonial tunic and ochre cloak, luminous musical ribbon wings forming broad loops, brass handbell and visible luminous soundwave rings and wrapped forearms; ochre navy gold palette.

### Iman — Techno-Magic

Home: Creasehaven. Visual brief: adult masculine fairy with warm brown skin, shoulder-length ivory locs tied back, coral circuit-patterned mantle over loose black trousers, angular segmented mechanical wings white and coral, glowing coral-white holographic control panel in hands; advanced technology magic; coral white ink palette.

### Sile — Water & Shapeflow

Home: The Glassmere. Visual brief: adult pale freckled woman, black chin-length blunt bob, lime-green eyes, long burgundy mariner apron over cream utility shirt and trousers, broad iridescent translucent water-fan wings in deep red and green, flowing water and flexible shapeflow magical liquid ribbon held between palms; burgundy chartreuse cream palette.

### Nemi — Wild Animal Magic

Home: The Lantern Orchard. Visual brief: adult deep-brown-skinned woman, long white rope braid, teal floor-length embroidered coat, black boots, enormous pale moon-moth wings with rust eyespots, glowing fox, owl and moth animal spirits circling a carved wooden lantern; teal rust and moonwhite palette.

## Campaign adversaries

### The Tollweaver

Junction: Gate of Hanging Roads. Threat to passage: Bind every route to a toll paid in years of life.

### Archivist Hush

Junction: The Unremembered Vault. Threat to memory: Erase histories so all dimensions believe they have always belonged to the archive.

### Caldrum the Still

Junction: The Unmoving Foundry. Threat to time: Freeze every world's future at the moment of his coronation.

### Broker Velis

Junction: The Contract Bazaar. Threat to consent: Purchase the names of dimensions through deceptive contracts and own their inhabitants.

### The Morrow Engine

Junction: The Borrowed Dawn. Threat to possibility: Consume every possible tomorrow to fuel one endless imperial present.

### Chancellor Aurex

Junction: The Hall of One Face. Threat to identity: Replace each world's people with obedient reflections of a single approved identity.

### The Crowned Covenant

Junction: The Loomheart. Threat to freedom: Fuse the stolen powers into a living law that prevents disagreement by abolishing free choice.

## Style

Vivid modern-anime fantasy illustration, rich saturated color, clean contours, strong cinematic contrast, prismatic light, and readable character silhouettes. No pre-existing franchise costumes, logos, or character likenesses are required. The cast are adults. Preserve the seven distinct magical domains while inventing new story details.
