# Format and first experiment

Google's [Photo Sphere XMP reference](https://developers.google.com/streetview/spherical-metadata) describes a full sphere as equirectangular 360° x 180°. GPano metadata includes ProjectionType=equirectangular, UsePanoramaViewer=True, full and cropped pixel dimensions, and zero crop offsets when the JPEG covers the full sphere. Metadata helps compatible apps identify a sphere, but does not validate imagery or guarantee specific app support. Google Photos on Android XR documents support for panorama/360 media; exact recognition of a generated file must be tested independently.

On 2026-11-15, the Storyverse God Observatory prototype generated a 1774 x 887 PNG. It was converted to JPEG and tagged, then mapped to a sphere and sampled at front, left, right, rear, up, and down. The rear join showed two awkwardly adjacent crystal posts, and the floor pinched at the lower pole. It had not been tried in Google Photos or Aura. Broad lesson: even a true 2:1 tagged image can still have poor seam geometry and pole distortions; inspect rather than equating correct dimensions with success. The image is mono, not stereoscopic. Avoid reproducing or exposing the user's private experiment in public copies.
