# The Two-Moon Crossing

*Agent-written adaptation of the recorded active continuity. This is literary presentation, not deterministic tool-generated prose.*

The canals met beneath two moons. One shone red over the remembered glass harbor; the other cast blue light across the floating garden. Where Mara and Orrin had once kept separate ferries, Marin now stood with both histories: building the glass boat, planting the garden, and remembering each life without pretending they had always been the same.

The traveler asked Marin to follow the blue moon.

Under its light, the ferrymaster guided the glass boat toward a stand of luminous reeds. Their brightness turned the canal into a passage between the two remembered worlds. The garden belonged to one history and the boat to another, but here they could carry the same traveler.

The crossing did not erase where either world had come from. It gave them somewhere new to go.
