# Independent extension test: The Two-Moon Crossing

An evaluating agent authored two original source worlds, their crossover decisions and the alternate narration, then invoked the real headless CLIs in separate processes. No external model API, private archive or personal documents were used.

## Crossover and chronological report

Glass Harbor remembered Mara and a red moon; Forest Canal remembered Orrin and a blue moon. An explicit mapping fused their counterparts into Marin, with one inventory coin and two retained source histories. Laws, time alignment and the moon/coin conflicts were decided in the supplied plan. Both original session files remained byte-identical. The crossover had a fresh world identity and preserved both complete source export packages through a further export/import.

The player first followed the red moon, then restored a checkpoint and chose blue. The deterministic narrative report correctly placed the blue voyage in current continuity and the red voyage in a separate abandoned-history section. The portable v3 export also produced a verified active local chronology. This report organizes recorded agent-authored prose; it does not claim to generate a new literary story or invent missing source events.

## Large-document initialization

Two synthetic UTF-8 documents were 1,196,038 and 1,196,039 bytes, split into 586 chunks. Only the first two chunks were initially reviewed: **0.341% coverage**. A scoped partial initialization preserved `partial:true`, `incomplete:true` and the unreviewed count in the new world. It did not claim complete reading.

For the full-coverage test, a deterministic recognizer fetched every remaining chunk and verified it as exact, deliberately repeated synthetic padding. This tests streaming, provenance and review bookkeeping—not comprehension of large books. A conflicting single-moon interpretation was recorded; export without explicit partial permission remained blocked while unresolved. The agent resolved it as two moons, then a full initialization succeeded with the compact source provenance attached to the world.

## Outcomes and limits

- 1798 real CLI invocations in the final clean run.
- Fresh crossover, source immutability, entity fusion, preserved multi-parent lineage, portable continuation and correct branch-separated narrative all passed.
- Bounded document reads, explicit partial coverage, conflict gate/resolution and source-initialized world export all passed.
- No implementation blocker found. Source excerpts/claims are agent-authored; quote matching proves a quote occurs, not that every paraphrase or inference is justified. In this fixture, one generic “harbor” paraphrase was broader than the original “forest canal”; the final initialized facts used the precise source wording. The tool cannot certify semantic comprehension or automatically identify all contradictions.
- Raw evidence contains fictional spoilers and local test paths. Publish this report/summary and the generated narrative, not raw source inputs or unreviewed arbitrary documents.
