# The Ferry Garden

*Agent-written adaptation of selected recorded saga milestones. Connective phrasing is literary interpretation, not additional recovered history.*

Ada began with a promise: the flood-damaged ferry landing would have a garden the town could share. Five years later, she welcomed Lio. The child grew up beside the landing, and by year eighteen they were planting the first riverside beds together.

The promise outlived its beginning. In year twenty-five, Lio welcomed Mira. There were now three generations connected to the same unfinished place. Ada died in year forty. Lio chose to continue the work, and the garden passed into Lio and Mira’s care.

At year sixty, they opened it to the town.

The record does not preserve every season, every difficult conversation or every ordinary day between those moments. It preserves a promise, two births, shared work, a death and an opening. The garden was not the whole of their lives. It was the thread they carried together.
