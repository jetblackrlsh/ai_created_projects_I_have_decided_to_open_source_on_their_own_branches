🎲 CRPG Turn-Based Rules, Modes & 10 Win Conditions

🪄 Full Party or Solo Hero Mode

Play with both Fairy Mage (Fairy Wand Mage) and Sword Knight (Magic Sword Paladin), or choose Solo: Fairy Mage Only or Solo: Sword Knight Only in the top-left panel! Solo heroes receive a +1 Max AP & +8 Max HP blessing.

🎯 10 Randomized Win Conditions

Random dungeons automatically roll one of 10 Win Condition types (Defeat all or X enemies, Defeat X or all Killer Frogs / Moles, Collect all or X treasures / Rubies / Gold Bricks / Flowers, or hybrid Defeat + Collect quests!). You can also pick any goal manually or click 🎲 to reroll!

🧱 Random Brick Dungeon Generator

Click 🎲 New Random Dungeon anytime to rearrange the exact same Brick pieces (grey bridge bricks, blue frog water, brown mole gardens, 11 rubies/gems, 3 gold bricks, and 7 jewel flowers) into new dungeon configurations!

🐸 Killer Frogs & Garden Mole Monsters

Snipe Killer Frogs across the water with Fairy Mage's 9-stud Fairy Wand or Sword Knight's Sonic Wave, and use Sword Knight's Baseplate Tremor Slam or Fairy Mage's Crystal Starburst to unearth burrowed Garden Moles!
