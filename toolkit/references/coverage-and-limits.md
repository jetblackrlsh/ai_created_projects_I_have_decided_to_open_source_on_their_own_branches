# Scope and limitations

This original implementation was developed while studying general principles from *How to Draw Comics the Marvel Way*. Study was organized into 53 aggregated lesson families, not 53 publisher-numbered exercises. The source contains demonstrations and open-ended practice, not a finite workbook. Private study logs and story-specific adaptations are not included.

## Supported
Editable SVG paths and JSON scenes, stable IDs, deterministic exports and local geometry/pixel invariance tests. Architectural perspective and separate construction mannequins. Bounded contour figures, heads, varied body proportions, staged actions and paired contact studies. Panel layouts, captions, original storyboards, stroke families and black-mass studies.

## Limits
No continuous anatomical 3D camera, realistic muscle deformation, cloth simulation or IK. Figures remain geometric and poses/expressions are bounded. This is not naturalistic master-level illustration or arbitrary prompt-to-image generation. Physical brush, glass and mirror exercises were digital analogues; not every printed pose was copied. A six-panel analogous sequence was not a blind-before-solution exercise. No model was retrained.

Confidence is high in repeatable export and editability within tested bounds, moderate in schematic staging, lower in anatomy, subtle acting, extreme foreshortening and interlocking hands. Unit tests do not prove visual quality. Inspect every new output.
