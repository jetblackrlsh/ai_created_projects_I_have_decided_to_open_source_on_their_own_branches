# Required visual-style review

This is a bounded drawing toolkit, not a model trained to reproduce a publisher or artist. A valid SVG, a named module, or a passing test does not establish visual style. For the default heroic American-comic workflow, inspect the artwork itself before presenting it as meeting the brief.

## Before a full production

Inspect a suitable reference that the requester has supplied or accepted when available. Otherwise choose an original or lawfully accessible reference and state that it is a proposed target, not a user-approved one. Record relevant construction, proportion, depth, expressive gesture and ink/value traits without copying its characters or composition.

Draw a representative demanding panel and retain its construction view: gesture, landmarks and solids must actually inform the finished shapes. Use the shared figure/head, perspective and inking modules where appropriate, recording the functions and parameters that produced visible geometry. Bespoke SVG paths remain appropriate for clothing, props, species and unsupported forms. They are not a bypass for the requested visual language.

Inspect at intended display size and record specific observations:
- Proportions, age and identity fit the brief; no unrequested mascot/chibi substitution.
- Visible forms connect convincingly; supporting feet and hands contact their intended surfaces or objects.
- Gesture, expression and camera choice communicate the action; avoid repeating a front-facing emblem.
- Line hierarchy and shadow masses describe volume rather than merely outlining flat fills.
- Shared perspective, overlap and near/middle/far staging create deliberate depth.
- Nonhuman subjects read as the intended species or design; do not force humanoid anatomy or muscular adults on every subject.
- Narrative clarity and requested tone survive the treatment.

Use a separate image-only reader when available. Ask what style, age/species, action and spatial relationships they see before revealing the intended result. Compare against the brief afterward. If a preview was rejected, revise and resolve that specific concern before scaling up; never declare the style fixed from engineering tests alone.

## Evidence and honesty

Retain the representative image SHA256, construction/source references, visible module/path provenance, reviewer observations, unresolved issues and review status. Review every final panel for drift. Changes to reviewed images invalidate the old review. Code can enforce presence, file hashes and status, but cannot certify that a review is truthful or that pixels match a style.

Track style, story readability, text readability and export tests separately. Claims should say original stylized American heroic-comic principles, not naturalistic Marvel-equivalent execution. The included construction studies demonstrate bounded tools, not an approved finished-story style benchmark.
