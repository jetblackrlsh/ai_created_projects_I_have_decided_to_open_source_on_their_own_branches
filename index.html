      Roll for Shoes — Rules

      Roll for Shoes is a tabletop RPG micro-system created by Ben Wray (CC0 1.0):

      1. Say what you do and roll a number of D6s, determined by the level of relevant skill you have.
      2. If the sum of your roll is higher than an opposing roll, the thing you wanted to happen, happens.
      3. At start, you have only one skill: Do Anything 1.
      4. If you roll all 6s, you get a new skill specific to the action, one level higher than the one you used.
      5. For every roll you fail, you get 1 XP.
      6. XP can be used to change a die into a 6 for advancement purposes only.

      Solo Sandbox & Oracle

      • Type anything: Describe your action freely. Your best matching skill is auto-selected, or click any skill in the sidebar.
      • Solo Oracle: Ask Yes/No questions with odds (Unlikely, 50/50, Likely), or roll Spark and Twist / NPC prompts whenever you want inspiration.
      • Advancing with XP: After any roll where not all dice are 6s, click individual dice (1 XP each) or Flip All to 6s to unlock a higher-level skill.

      All 6s! New Skill Unlocked

      Your Do Anything 1 rolled all 6s! Name your new Level 2 skill specific to what you just did:

      Example: "Pick Brass Locks", "Sweet-Talk Guards", "Vault Chandeliers"
