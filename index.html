✨ How to Play Lumenweave: The Unbound Accord

1. Turn-Based Spell Combat & Battle Log

Each turn, your Waykeeper Fairy restores 3 Mana Crystals (💎) and draws 4 Spell Cards from their 8-card personal deck. Every action is recorded in the 📜 Turn-by-Turn Battle Combat Log at the bottom of the arena.

2. Status Effects & Elemental Mechanics

  • 🛡️ Shield: Blocks incoming villain damage this round (Iman retains up to 10 Shield between turns).
  • 🔥 Burn / Toxin / Venom: Deals damage over time to the villain at the start of their turn, then decreases by 1.
  • 💫 Inspire: Adds bonus damage to every Attack spell your fairy casts (`+1 DMG per stack`).
  • 🌿 Regen: Restores HP at the end of your turn, then decreases by 1.
  • 🌙 Weaken: Reduces attack damage by 2 per stack.
  • 🕸️ Entangle: Vines/cages reduce the villain's attack damage by 35%!
  • 🪞 Reflect: Bounces damage back at the villain when they hit you!

3. Story Mode Level Codes & Deck Progression

After beating each villain in Story Mode, you upgrade 1 card per fairy (`Spell+`) and receive an updated 🔑 Level Code (e.g. `LUMEN-L3-ST-03`) that encodes your unlocked chapter, chosen Waykeeper fairy/fairies, and exact upgraded cards!
