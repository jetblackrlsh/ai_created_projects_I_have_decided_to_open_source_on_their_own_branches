How to Play The Elf Game Solo

1. The Core Loop (No GM Needed!)

Playing solo is like having a conversation with yourself and the dice:

  1. Set the scene: Use Inspire Me (Settings/Creatures/Twists) or write a short note in the chat box.
  2. Ask the Oracle: When you want to know something a GM would normally decide (e.g., "Is the bridge guarded?"), type your question and click Yes/No or How Many.
  3. Take action: When your character tries something risky, click an Ability Check (STR, DEX, CON, INT, WIS, CHA) or Attack. The app does all the math and explains why you succeeded or failed!
  4. Write what happens: Log the outcome as a story note and keep going.

2. How Elf Game Checks Work (Roll-Under D20)

Unlike games where you want a high roll, The Elf Game is a roll-under system: lower rolls are better!

  • Ability Tests: Roll 1D20, subtract any bonuses (like a Tradesman's Level), and add any Difficulty penalty. If the result is less than or equal to (≤) your Ability Score, you Succeed!
  • Natural 1 & Natural 20: Rolling a 1 on the D20 is always a Success. Rolling a 20 is always a Failure.
  • Attack Rolls: Roll 1D20 and subtract your Melee or Ranged score. If the result is ≤ Target's Armor Class (AC), you hit and deal 1D6 damage!
  • Armor Class (AC): Starts at 10 and goes down with good stats, armor, and defending. A lower AC is harder to hit!
  • Saving Throws: Roll against your Saving Throw score (CON + WIS Adj + Level) to resist magic or survive dropping to 0 HP.

3. Classes & Stances

  • Fighter: Rolls an extra 1D6 for HP at each Level beyond 0, and can use combat Actions to Attack.
  • Tradesman: Subtracts their Level from all non-Attack Ability Tests, and can use Attacks in combat to perform Actions.
  • Magical Stance: May use Actions to cast Spells.
  • Mundane Stance: Gains +1 to HP, Speed, Saving Throw, Melee, and Ranged, and -1 to Armor Class.

4. Chat Slash Commands (Optional Shortcuts)

  • /str, /dex, /con, /int, /wis, /cha, /save — Roll a check (e.g. /dex dodge the trap)
  • /melee or /ranged — Roll an attack (e.g. /melee swing at goblin)
  • /enemy — Roll an enemy attack against your AC (e.g. /enemy Goblin lunges)
  • /oracle — Ask the Yes/No Oracle (e.g. /oracle Is the chest trapped?)
  • /howmany — Ask the How Many Oracle (e.g. /howmany guards?)
  • /inspire [category] — Roll random inspiration
