# Original reference guide

Source: seven original Microsoft Paint character templates supplied by Jet Black on 2026-11-13. These are original user drawings, not AI examples. Keep the original bytes intact. The descriptions below aid selection, not replace viewing.

- `../assets/01-base.png`: front-facing broad flat-topped head, big nested oval eyes, ears, simple nose/mouth, narrow waist, broad hips; unfinished arms and upper legs. Primary proportion reference.
- `../assets/02-striped-monster.png`: same modular base with angular eyes, jagged face edges and zigzag body stripes.
- `../assets/03-circuit-robot.png`: rectangular nested eyes, side antenna/bolt shapes and circular circuit nodes joined by right-angle lines.
- `../assets/04-catlike.png`: pointed ears, half-lidded eyes, whiskers, collar and a small striped tail.
- `../assets/05-masked-hero.png`: eye mask, broad chest/collar shapes, belt and hanging costume strips.
- `../assets/06-monster-armor.png`: pointed eye interiors, jagged fur-like edges and patterned shoulder shapes.
- `../assets/07-posed-figure.png`: landscape framed drawing, same head/eye language with eyelashes and fuller lips; raised bent arm and hand on hip, strongly exaggerated torso. Shows the style supports poses, not only a static base.

All seven are primarily black outlines on white without rendered color or shading. Avoid mistaking raster line contours for conventional tile/sprite pixel art.

## Calibration
Initial version derived from the originals and their supplied explanation. On 2026-11-13, Jet approved the first generated goblin example as very accurate and like something he would draw. Treat that as positive calibration, not permission to replace the original reference drawings or claim every future image matches. Preserve the same simple handmade contours and proportion language; refine only in response to specific observed issues or user feedback.

- `../assets/approved-male/06-fairy.png`: revised male fairy with a flat blank chest garment.
- `../assets/approved-male/07-posed-figure.png`: male flexing pose.

Jet did not approve the first male superhero and fairy adaptations: their chests remained too shapely or implied cleavage. Do not use those rejected attempts as references. For future male superhero/fairy drawings, retain original motifs but use flat uninterrupted chest areas, no cleavage-like V lines or curved breast contours, and straighter torso silhouettes. Jet subsequently approved the revised flat-chested superhero and fairy versions on 2026-11-13; these are the versions bundled above. The original `06-monster-armor.png` is the design Jet calls the fairy; preserve its patterned shoulders and side wing forms.
