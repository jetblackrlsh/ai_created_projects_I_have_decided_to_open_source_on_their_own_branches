How this game works

Checks use 1d20 + ability modifier + situational modifiers against a displayed difficulty. A score’s modifier is floor((score − 10) ÷ 2). Damage is fixed, not rolled. Power costs, risks, and odds are shown before you commit.

Resources are limited. Powers are not automatic solutions. The adventure offers ordinary approaches too, but you can still lose. Save files and characters are portable; autosaves stay in this browser and may be cleared by browser settings.

This is an authored offline game, not a live AI game master. Available actions are deliberate choices. Power themes are interpreted only through their configured effects.
