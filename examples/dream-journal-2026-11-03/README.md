# Echoes in the Static: Dream Journal (11/03/2026)

A complete **Moss-Keeper Painterly Anime** adaptation of the `Dream Journal 11/03/2026` entry (private source document; identifier omitted), built using the upgraded `webgl-canvas-story-animator` skill.

## Scenes Adapted

1. **`S01` — The Storyverse Void & Avatar of Nova**: Inducing full lucidity through sleep paralysis into the sparkling purple Lucid Light of the Storyverse void and brainstorming a story with an avatar of Nova.
2. **`S02` — The Hunter's Lodge & Cosmic Static**: A bathroom argument and broken glass bottle lead into a timber hunter's lodge where a family of human-sized felt Muppet relatives in flannel, white tees, jeans, and camo stare unblinkingly at CRT white-noise static—linked upon waking to the Cosmic Microwave Background radiation from the Big Bang.
3. **`S03` — Booth Hide-and-Seek & The Hourly Sleep-Debt Hotel**: Tuning out a ranting acquaintance to play a mobile game in a restaurant booth while the acquaintance searches under tables like hide-and-seek, followed by a bilingual European desk clerk at an hourly hotel where meals are paid in hours slept and a deathly exhausted clerk embodies the peril of sleep debt.
4. **`S04` — The Pop-Up Grimoire of Living Relics**: Passing over an overpriced trinket to open an illuminated pop-up grimoire whose 3D components lift directly off the page and lock together into a genuine celestial relic.
5. **`S05` — The 9:45 AM Milkshake Line & The Lucidity Cloud**: Standing in an outdoor mall ice cream line with Ashley at `9:45 AM`, summoning a partial-lucidity storm cloud to morph the plaza into Handel's parking lot before Ashley's request for cocoa-mint shakes fizzles the cloud out, and an old college QA acquaintance remarks on Ashley's love of cocoa-mint despite never having met her and her waking-life dislike of mint.
6. **`S06` — The Five-Star Cheesecake-Chicken-Ranch Banquet & Waking Alarm**: Expanding into a grand crystal-chandelier banquet hall where the QA acquaintance orders crispy chicken tenders sandwiched between two slices of cheesecake drenched in ranch dressing—waking Jet via his morning alarm at the exact split-second before the first bite.
