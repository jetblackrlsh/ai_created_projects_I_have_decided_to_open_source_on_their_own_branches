## 2. Zero Narrator-Dialogue Repetition Verification

- Every line in `script/lines.json` has been checked to guarantee **strict chronological lockstep**:

  - **Characters (`Jet`, `Nova`, `Daughter`, `Grandmother`, `Concierge`, `Ashley`, `QA Acquaintance`, `Horrified Diner`)** never repeat what the Narrator just described; instead, their dialogue advances the plot, reveals rules, or triggers the next visual transformation.
