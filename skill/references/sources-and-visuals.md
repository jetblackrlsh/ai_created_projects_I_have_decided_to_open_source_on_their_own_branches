# Sources and visual planning

Record `source_id`, original URL/document ID, title, creator, publication/access dates, rights basis, excerpt boundaries, local path and SHA-256. For screenshots record capture date and whether crop/redaction was applied. Keep unredacted sensitive screenshots private and outside any publication package. Capture only permitted sources, not personal computer sessions without authorization.

Assign each scene a source ID and spoken claim. Label conceptual illustrations as illustrations. Use a source's exact chart labels, baselines, units and time period. Cropping must not hide qualifying context. Never stretch a screenshot or fake a quote. Use a generated original background for atmosphere, not documentary evidence.

At 360p, use 20–24px headings/body and no more than three short bullets. Full-page screenshots are establishing views only; enlarge the relevant region for discussion. Give each key quote/evidence scene at least its spoken duration and enough pause to read. A host can be left/right or absent when evidence needs the whole frame. Prefer a new shot over crowded overlays. Source credits remain legible, with full links in accompanying metadata.

Suggested project layout: `script.json`, `sources.json`, `host/*.png`, `evidence/*`, `audio/*`, `manifest.json`, `captions.srt`, `youtube_metadata.md`, `qa.json`, `video.mp4`. Use relative paths in a downloadable project, not workspace-specific paths.
